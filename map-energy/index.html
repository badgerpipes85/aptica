Powerwall diagnostics and deeper insight

MAP Energy is built for users who want more than a simple overview. Where supported, Powerwall diagnostic tools can help expose deeper information about your system and make troubleshooting easier.

  • Powerwall site information and useful system details.
  • Diagnostic features for compatible Powerwall systems.
  • Local pairing support where available.
  • Designed around real user feedback and requested features.

The goal is simple: give connected energy users a clearer picture of what their system is doing, why it is doing it, and how to automate the repetitive parts.

  • Less guesswork.
  • More useful notifications.
  • More flexible automation.
  • Better visibility across battery, solar, grid and EV usage.

Why MAP Energy?

Default energy apps are useful, but many connected home energy users want more visibility, smarter notifications and more flexible automations. MAP Energy is built to fill that gap with practical tools for Powerwall owners, EV charging users, solar households and smart tariff users across supported regions.

How it works

  1. Download MAP Energy on iOS or Android.
  2. Create or sign in to your MAP Energy account.
  3. Connect supported energy services.
  4. Monitor, understand and automate your home energy setup.
