Automations

Your energy system can react for you

MAP Energy automations connect events happening around your home with useful actions. Build simple rules or combine conditions to make your Powerwall and connected energy setup respond automatically.

1 Something happens An EV starts charging, battery SOC changes, a schedule arrives or an energy condition is met.
→
2 MAP Energy checks Apply configured conditions, time windows and blocking rules before anything changes.
→
3 Your action runs Adjust supported Powerwall settings, send a notification or trigger Home Assistant.
