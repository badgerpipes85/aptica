Connect your supplier in the app

Connect or update your supplier through the MAP Energy mobile app. The portal uses the connected site’s tariff information; it does not provide a separate supplier sign-in form.

Supported integrations include Octopus Energy and EDF in the UK, and Amber Electric in Australia. The available rates and features depend on your account and tariff. Intelligent Octopus Go and EDF Smart Bolt-On features require their corresponding eligible account setup; Amber pricing does not imply the same smart charging events.

If you use a custom tariff, maintain it in the app. Import and export can have separate rates and must be available separately for their respective cost calculations.

Read the Supplier tab

Import is on the left and Export on the right on wider screens. On phones they stack in that order. Each available card shows its tariff, rate graph, current rate, lowest/highest rates and a standing charge when provided.

Hover over a bar with your mouse, or tap it on a touch screen, to see its time and rate to one decimal place. The rate display temporarily switches to the selected interval, then returns to the current rate. There is no separate Explore rates slider.

Rates use the site’s time zone and the currency supplied with the data. Missing rates remain unavailable; genuine zero and negative rates remain visible. UK rate cards display VAT-inclusive prices.

For an available Octopus Agile tariff, the Agile control opens its additional graph/table view. History applies dated rates to energy readings to calculate costs; Supplier is the place to inspect today’s rate information.

Understand update times

The page’s Loaded time records when the portal retrieved that view. It is different from the age of the underlying supplier data.

  • Supplier rates checked shows when a supported supplier rate lookup was performed.
  • Saved rates shows when MAP Energy last saved those rates. Opening the page does not change that timestamp.
  • If a supplier refresh fails, the page can show saved data with a message explaining the fallback.
  • If only an older day’s rates are available, the card identifies the date and does not label them as current prices.

Open Rate information on a card to see these details. Use Refresh to try again, and check the supplier connection in the app if the data remains unavailable.

EDF Power Perks in the portal

For an eligible EDF site registered for Power Perks, the Power Perks icon appears on Live. Open it to review upcoming and previous events, their status, available free-energy totals and estimated savings.

The event view follows the app’s status conventions, including Ready, In Progress, Missed and Unavailable. Energy and savings depend on available readings and tariff information; an unavailable figure is not treated as zero.

Where the portal offers Confirm participation for a past event, review the event and confirmation carefully. The portal warns that this confirmation cannot be undone. It records participation in MAP Energy; use EDF’s own information to confirm your eligibility and bill treatment.

Registered sites can also create custom Power Perks automations for Event Announced, Free Electricity Starts or Free Electricity Ends. Configure dedicated EDF Smart Bolt-On and automatic smart charging behaviour in the mobile app.

For how recorded events affect historical energy costs and usage percentages, see free and smart History overlays.

Switching supplier

A Switching supplier? section can appear for supported UK supplier connections when the referral offer is enabled. It shows the current EDF referral information and opens EDF’s site.

Follow the offer link for the current terms and eligibility. Opening the link does not change your supplier connection in MAP Energy. Update that connection in the app after a switch.
