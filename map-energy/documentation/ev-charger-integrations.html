EV Charger Integrations

Connect a supported EV charger to MAP Energy so charging can become part of your wider home energy system — including Powerwall protection, smart tariff automations and EV charging history.

Why connect your EV charger?

Your EV can be one of the largest electrical loads in your home. Knowing when it starts and stops charging allows MAP Energy to understand that load and coordinate your Powerwall around it.

A supported charger connection can allow MAP Energy to identify EV charging activity, display EV charging information and use charging events as automation triggers.

Protect your stored energy

EV charging & smart tariffs

EV charger information becomes even more useful when MAP Energy is also connected to a supported smart electricity tariff.

Some smart tariffs can schedule EV charging dynamically. This means charging may begin outside the normal fixed off-peak period.

MAP Energy can combine supported tariff information with EV charging detection to help coordinate your Powerwall around these charging periods.

How does MAP Energy detect charging?

MAP Energy monitors available charger information to determine when a charging session has started or stopped.

Charging does not always change instantaneously. Chargers can briefly report low power, pause during a session or take time to update their status.

MAP Energy therefore applies charging detection logic rather than treating every individual power reading as a new charging event.

i
This helps avoid false triggers

A brief drop or spike in charger power should not necessarily be treated as the end or beginning of a charging session. MAP Energy confirms charging state before generating supported EV start and stop events.

EV charging in Live & History

Where supported charging power is available, MAP Energy can show the EV as part of your live home energy picture.

This makes it easier to understand how much of the power currently entering your home is being used by the vehicle rather than normal household demand.

Supported EV charging information can also be incorporated into History, helping separate EV consumption from the rest of your household energy use.

Does MAP Energy control my EV charger?

Connecting an EV charger does not automatically mean that MAP Energy can remotely control every function of that charger.

Charger manufacturers expose different capabilities. Some integrations may provide charging status and power information without providing direct charging control.

MAP Energy only uses the functionality supported by the individual integration.

Charger capabilities vary

Do not assume that features available with one charger will also be available with another. Available MAP Energy options are determined by the capabilities of the connected charger and integration.

What if my charger disconnects?

Third-party charger integrations depend on the services provided by the charger manufacturer.

Authentication can expire, manufacturer services can become temporarily unavailable, or an account connection can be revoked.

If MAP Energy cannot retrieve the charging information required by an EV automation, that can prevent the expected EV event from being detected.

If charging is no longer appearing correctly, check the EV charger connection in MAP Energy and reconnect the integration if requested.

EV charger questions

Do I need an EV charger integration to use MAP Energy?

No. EV charger integrations are optional. MAP Energy can still be used with your Powerwall and other supported services without connecting an EV charger.

Why should MAP Energy know when my EV is charging?

EV charging can create a large household load. Detecting it allows MAP Energy to distinguish that load and lets you create automations that respond when charging starts or stops.

Can MAP Energy stop my Powerwall charging my EV?

MAP Energy can use supported Powerwall controls to change how your Powerwall behaves when EV charging is detected. A common strategy is to preserve the Powerwall while the vehicle is charging and restore normal settings when the session finishes.

Does MAP Energy control the charging schedule?

Not necessarily. Smart charging schedules may be managed by your charger, vehicle or electricity supplier. MAP Energy can respond to supported charging events without needing to be the service that originally scheduled the charge.

Why does charging sometimes take a few minutes to appear?

Charger integrations update at different speeds, and MAP Energy also confirms charging state to reduce false start or stop events caused by short power fluctuations.

My charger isn't listed. Can it be added?

Possibly. New integrations depend on whether the charger manufacturer provides a suitable and reliable way for MAP Energy to retrieve the information required. Suggestions for additional charger integrations are welcome.

Need help?

If your EV charger is connected but charging is not being detected correctly, check the charger connection in MAP Energy first.

If the connection appears healthy but the problem continues, contact MAP Energy support and include your charger brand, the approximate time of a recent charging session and a description of what you expected to happen.

This information can help us compare the charger data with MAP Energy's EV detection and automation history.
