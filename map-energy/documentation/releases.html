Version 1.7.0
More insight. More control. Meet Mappy.
Latest Release

MAP Energy 1.7.0 brings a refreshed app experience, richer energy history, EDF PowerPerks and more ways to manage your Powerwall. Premium users can also monitor and control their setup through the Web Portal.

iOS Android Feature Release
Release notes
  • Meet Mappy: A new welcome experience and Mappy hub bring setup status, useful links and plain-language insights into what your home energy system is doing.
  • Mappy Awards: Track your progress and view achievement badges in the app.
  • Refreshed Live view: Open your supplier directly from its logo, with clearer indicators for off-grid operation and disabled grid charging.
  • EDF PowerPerks: Eligible registered EDF users can view available free-electricity events, participation information and available energy and savings summaries.
  • PowerPerks automations: Create supported automations around EDF PowerPerks events, with conditions and Powerwall actions to suit your setup.
  • Supplier tariff sync to Tesla: Keep your selected Tesla site’s utility rate plan aligned with supported supplier or custom import and export rates saved in MAP Energy. Enabling sync replaces the existing Tesla rate plan; Tesla pricing constraints apply.
  • More detailed History charts: Explore import and export readings, energy usage, rates and costs for individual chart intervals. Rebuild supported historical days to add available detail.
  • Usage-by-rate rings: See how imported energy is split between Free, Smart EV, Off-peak and Standard usage across day, week, month, billing and year views, where the relevant rate and slot data is available.
  • Historic IOG slots: Import supported Octopus data files to add historical smart charging slots to your app history.
  • Premium Web Portal: Access Live, Supplier, History and Automations from a browser using web access configured in the app.
  • Powerwall control from the portal: Change supported Backup Reserve, operating mode and export settings, and open detailed views for all five energy nodes. Backup Reserve uses the app’s 0–80%, then 100%, control.
  • Portal History and supplier rates: Retrieve history live from Tesla, explore interval rates and costs, and view usage rings, standing charges and recorded free or smart slots where available. Import stays on the left and export on the right.
  • Portal automation management: Create and edit custom automations, pause and resume supported rules, and delete rules with confirmation. The portal uses the same saved automations as the app.
  • Refinements and fixes: Improved site naming, graph colours and chart-point readings in the portal, plus historical cost allocation and EDF standing-charge retrieval.

Features depend on your connected equipment, supplier, region and Free or Premium access. Mobile availability follows each store’s rollout. See the Web Portal guide for browser setup and supported controls.

Web Portal update

27 September 2026 · Website and service update

This update is available through the Web Portal and is separate from the mobile app version notes below.

  • Refreshed Live view, named-site selection, supported Backup / Mode / Export controls and consistent energy formatting.
  • Detail views for all five energy nodes, graph-point readings, app-aligned Grid / EV colours and the 0–80%, then 100%, reserve slider.
  • Eligible EDF Power Perks events, participation information and available energy/savings summaries.
  • Supplier import/export cards, one-decimal hover/tap rates, clearer data timestamps, Agile views and the supported supplier-switching section.
  • Tesla History retrieval, day navigation, import usage rings, recorded free/smart overlays and daily detail.
  • Historical cost allocation, including midnight-boundary readings, and corrected EDF standing-charge retrieval.
  • Portal creation, editing, supported pause/resume and confirmed deletion of custom automations, using the same saved rules as the app.

Version 1.6.0Feature Release
⌄
iOS and Android · Store availability announced 24 June 2026

A major update to suppliers, forecasts, automations, energy history and the Live experience.

  • Added EDF integration with Smart Slots and Amber Electric supplier support.
  • Added solar forecasting using your own Solcast API key.
  • Added day-of-week settings for manual tariffs.
  • Added free-hour tariff overrides and billing-month views to History.
  • Added optional automation names, time windows and solar forecast above/below triggers.
  • Improved failure reporting for multiple automation actions and automatic retries.
  • Added Intelligent Octopus Go automation support without a supported EV charger, where charging is visible in home usage.
  • Added individual battery state of charge and estimated battery degradation.
  • Added optional grid import/export colours and consolidated Live controls.
  • Improved graphs, iPad and Android tablet layouts, Ideas Lab organisation, app permissions and setup guidance.
  • General interface, reliability and performance improvements.

Version 1.4.1Maintenance
⌄
iOS · 22 April 2026

A major improvement to the Tesla connection, with early diagnostics and better history handling.

  • Moved core Tesla Powerwall data and controls from the Owner API to a backend Fleet API connection.
  • Improved connection stability and reduced repeated sign-in.
  • Improved historical backfill and prevented collection before a valid supplier tariff was configured, avoiding incorrect zero-cost records.
  • Improved full-screen automation views.
  • Added firmware versions and connected inverter details.
  • Introduced early Premium Powerwall diagnostics using a local Powerwall Wi-Fi connection.
