iOS and Android releases

MAP Energy is developed for both iOS and Android. Occasionally a release may reach one store before the other because Apple and Google review and publish updates independently.

Found a problem after an update?

If something isn't working as expected after installing a MAP Energy release, contact support and include your device platform, app version and a brief description of the issue.
