More Premium features are coming

MAP Energy continues to evolve. Premium gives access to the advanced features available today as well as eligible Premium functionality introduced in future releases.

Premium Automations

Automations are one of the core benefits of MAP Energy Premium.

Instead of manually changing your Powerwall settings as your energy situation changes, MAP Energy can monitor supported events and perform your configured actions automatically.

Depending on your connected services, automations can respond to events such as EV charging, battery state of charge, schedules, electricity prices, solar conditions and supported smart tariff events.

Actions can include Powerwall controls, notifications and sending structured events to a connected Home Assistant instance.

Powerwall Diagnostic Data

Premium users can access MAP Energy's supported Powerwall diagnostic features.

Diagnostic data provides additional information beyond the standard Tesla cloud data available to MAP Energy.

Because this information comes from the Powerwall's diagnostic interface, an additional pairing process may be required before the feature can be used.

i
Initial pairing may require local access

Depending on your Powerwall, you may need to be at home and connected to the same local network as the Powerwall when completing the initial diagnostic connection.

MAP Energy Web Portal

Premium also includes access to the MAP Energy web portal.

The web portal complements the iOS and Android apps and provides access to supported MAP Energy information from a web browser.

As MAP Energy develops, additional web-based functionality can be introduced alongside the mobile experience.

How do I subscribe?

Premium subscriptions are purchased from within the MAP Energy app using the store associated with your device.

1
Open MAP Energy

Open MAP Energy on your iPhone, iPad or Android device.

2
Choose Premium

Open the Premium subscription option and review the plans available in your country.

3
Select monthly or annual

Choose the subscription option that suits you and confirm the purchase through the App Store or Google Play.

i
Check the store price before subscribing

The price displayed by the App Store or Google Play during purchase is the price applicable to your account and region.

Managing your subscription

MAP Energy Premium is an in-app subscription managed through the store where you purchased it.

Your subscription management, renewal and cancellation options are therefore available through your Apple App Store or Google Play account.

Cancelling a subscription prevents future renewal. Premium access normally remains available until the end of the subscription period you have already paid for.

Subscription questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes. MAP Energy Premium includes a 14-day free trial for eligible new subscriptions.

Why is the price in my app different from £3.99 or £39.99?

These are the UK prices. Apple and Google display the appropriate local price and currency for your store account, and pricing can vary between countries.

Do I have to subscribe annually?

No. You can choose between monthly and annual Premium subscription options.

Is the annual subscription cheaper?

At UK pricing, twelve monthly payments would cost £47.88. The £39.99 annual subscription therefore saves £7.89 compared with paying monthly for twelve months.

What happens if I cancel?

Your subscription will not renew for another billing period. Premium access normally remains active until the end of your current paid subscription period.

I subscribed but Premium isn't showing. What should I do?

Make sure the purchase completed successfully and that you are using the correct App Store or Google Play account. You can also use any available restore purchase option in MAP Energy. If Premium still does not appear, contact MAP Energy support.

Is Energy Manager available now?

Not yet. Energy Manager is an upcoming Premium feature and is currently in development.

Need help with Premium?

If you have purchased Premium and your subscription is not being recognised, or you have another subscription question, contact MAP Energy support.

When contacting support, it is helpful to include the platform you are using — iOS or Android — and a brief description of the problem.
