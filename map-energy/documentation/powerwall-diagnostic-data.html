Powerwall Diagnostic Data

MAP Energy Premium provides additional diagnostic information for compatible Tesla Powerwall systems beyond the standard cloud energy view. Available battery, system and solar information depends on your hardware and diagnostic connection.

Connect diagnostics

Open the diagnostic connection in the MAP Energy app and follow its pairing instructions. Initial pairing may require your phone to be on the same local network as the Powerwall and physical confirmation at the Powerwall when requested. Connecting your Tesla account alone may not complete diagnostic pairing.

If data is unavailable

Check your Premium access, local network connection and the pairing status in the app. Complete any requested confirmation, then check whether your system exposes the diagnostic information you need. Available readings vary by system.

For help, contact support@aptica.uk with your app version, platform, Powerwall model and the pairing error. See diagnostic troubleshooting or MAP Energy Premium for related guidance.
